# Figure captions (English, for the manuscript)

Figure 1 Cumulative number of WHO-prequalified finished pharmaceutical products (FPPs), 2002–2026. (A) Cumulative listing counts for all FPPs and for malaria, HIV/AIDS and tuberculosis products, from the 15 September 2026 listing snapshot (n = 25 annual observations). (B) Average annual percentage change (AAPC, % per year) with 95% confidence intervals from a single log-linear ordinary least-squares fit to each cumulative series; intervals are the primary t intervals. HIV/AIDS appears in the listing trajectory only; burden alignment is reported for malaria and tuberculosis. Source: WHO prequalification listing, snapshot 15 September 2026

Figure 2 Geographic concentration of certificate-holder countries, 15 September 2026 listing snapshot (648 reviewed FPP product rows, 29 holder countries). (A) Share of product rows for the ten leading holder countries with 95% descriptive bootstrap intervals (10 000 product-row resamples); the remaining 19 holder countries are tabulated in the supplementary material. (B) Disease-specific supply shares within therapeutic area for malaria (n = 98 product rows, 11 holder countries) and tuberculosis (n = 126 product rows, 11 holder countries), grouped as India, China and all other holder countries; shares are not derived from the full listing. FPP, finished pharmaceutical product

Figure 3 Alignment between country-level disease burden and disease-specific supply shares, latest available burden year per country. (A) Share of burden and of prequalified supply (%) accounted for by the top quartile of high-burden countries, for malaria (n = 100 countries; burden years 2006–2024) and tuberculosis (n = 193 countries; burden years 2004–2024). (B) Burden-weighted supply coverage (C) and the top-quartile coverage ratio (R25) on a 0–1 index for the same two diseases; the dotted line marks a value of one. Burden indicators are from the WHO Global Health Observatory and World Bank World Development Indicators; the two diseases differ in burden unit, country count and year range, so their misalignment indices are not compared with one another. C, burden-weighted supply coverage; R25, ratio of supply to burden share among the top quartile
